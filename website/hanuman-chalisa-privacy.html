Privacy Policy – Hanuman Chalisa with Meaning

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how the Hanuman Chalisa with Meaning app ("the app"), published by NineXm Infotech, handles information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

Your settings and progress (language, text size, theme, completed paths and mala counts) are saved only on your phone. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Internet access

The app does not request internet access and works fully offline.

Audio and notifications

Devotional music and sounds are built into the app and play offline, including in the background with a media notification. The app does not use your microphone and does not record or send any audio. If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your phone only.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
